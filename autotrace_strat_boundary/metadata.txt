description=Predicts stratigraphic boundary outcrop lines from dip/dip-direction outcrop points and a DEM (structure-contour method), stops them at faults, builds a geological map from the edited lines, and a 3D geological model with sections. 依構造等高線原理由露頭位態與 DEM 預判地層邊界線並產生地質圖。
about=For each outcrop point of a stratigraphic boundary (dip, dip direction, upper and lower unit codes) the boundary plane is intersected with the DEM, and the outcrop line is followed to both sides of the point.
    Points of the same boundary in the same fault block are joined end to end through an inverse-distance blend of their planes, and lines stop on faults.
    A second tool polygonizes the edited boundary lines with the faults and assigns units from the side of each boundary, producing a geology layer.
    A third tool builds a 3D geological model and geological sections from the geological map, DEM, attitudes and faults (implicit potential-field method), saved as GeoPackage (2D section sheet + 3D layers), DXF / OBJ / STL and an offline three.js 3D viewer.
    Includes a downloadable sample project (oblique-slip fault displacing a plunging fold) and an illustrated guide to the principle and operation.
    User interface and guide: English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語 (follows the QGIS locale, switchable in the plugin menu).
    Requires only QGIS (GDAL and NumPy shipped with QGIS).
